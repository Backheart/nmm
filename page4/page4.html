EMPOWERING YOUNG WOMEN

The Mariam Ndagire Film and Performing Arts Centre, in partnership with Bayimba Cultural Foundation presents Mariam Ndagire Theatre Residency. The residency is implemented under the project “Collaboration for Skilling and Innovation in the Theatre Industry”, which aims to strengthen professional theatre practice in Uganda through structured training, mentorship, and collaborative creation.

The programme is designed to empower young women, including refugees and persons with disabilities (PWDs), by providing practical theatre and performing arts training that combines artistic development with technical and vocational skills.

The Mariam Ndagire Theatre Residency is an intensive skills development programme that brings together emerging female theatre practitioners for a period of creative exploration, professional training, and collaborative theatre-making.

The residency integrates Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) approaches with artistic practice to address skills gaps within Uganda’s theatre sector. Through practical workshops, rehearsals, and mentorship, participants gain hands-on experience in theatre performance and production.

Participants work closely with experienced theatre practitioners and industry professionals while developing new work rooted in Ugandan stories and lived experiences.

The residency concludes with a public theatre production, presenting the work created during the programme, internship and participating in the Theatre and performing Arts festival.

What Participants Gain

Selected participants will benefit from:

  • Intensive training in theatre performance and production.
  • Practical skills development through a TVET-informed training approach.
  • Mentorship from experienced theatre practitioners.
  • Opportunities to collaborate with other emerging artists.
  • Exposure to professional rehearsal and production processes.
  • Participation in a final staged theatre production.
  • Certificate of participation upon completion of the programme.
