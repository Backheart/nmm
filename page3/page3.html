Mariam Ndagire Film and Performing Arts Centre

Mariam Ndagire Film and Performing Arts Centre (MNFPAC) is a premium training and mentorship initiative dedicated to empowering emerging filmmakers and young creatives across Uganda. Established in 2010 by acclaimed filmmaker and musical artist Mariam Ndagire, the centre was built on a bold mission: to elevate East Africa's entertainment industry by nurturing highly skilled, disciplined, and passionate talent.

HANDS ON!

Through hands-on workshops, intensive practical courses, and direct industry mentorship, MNFPAC provides comprehensive training across every stage of media creation:

  • Pre-Production & Storytelling: Screenwriting, project development, and producing.
  • Technical Production: Cinematography, lighting, and sound recording.
  • Post-Production: Non-linear video editing, color grading, and audio mastering.
  • Performing Arts: Acting for screen, stage and radio.

By bridging classroom learning with real-world set experience, MNFPAC continues to equip the next generation of Ugandan storytellers with the tools, discipline, and artistic vision needed to thrive in the global film landscape.

Hands-On Training & Real-World Set Experience

At MNFPAC, learning goes far beyond the classroom walls. Students are immersed directly into real production environments, getting hands-on experience with professional cinema equipment, field audio rigs, and set direction under the guidance of active industry leaders. Every student collaborates on actual film projects, learning the grit, coordination, and creative discipline required on a live set.

Proven Industry Success & Festival Recognition

The quality of training at MNFPAC speaks for itself on the Ugandan entertainment circuit. Student projects and Alumni films created through the centre’s programs have gone on to achieve major national and international acclaim:

  • Festival Nominations & Awards: Works produced by MNFPAC students have earned nominations and wins at prominent platforms including the Uganda Film Festival (UFF), the Pearl International Film Festival (PIFF), and the Zanzibar International Film Festival (ZIFF).
  • Alumni Impact: MNFPAC graduates continuously launch successful careers as award-winning directors, screenwriters, cinematographers, and actors across East Africa's booming film and television landscape.

The Path to Professional Excellence

  • Practical Fieldwork: Trainees execute real production shoots, taking charge of camera operating, lighting setups, sound capture, and scene execution.
  • Direct Industry Mentorship: Learn directly from established filmmakers who bring years of broadcast and theatrical experience into every session.
  • Annual Graduation & Showcase: Each annual program cycle culminates in a grand graduation ceremony. Students premiere their collaborative films to industry professionals, receive their credentials, and officially step into the industry as job-ready creators.

Whether you are looking to step behind the camera or command the stage, MNFPAC is where your creative journey transforms into a professional career.
