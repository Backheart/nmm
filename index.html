Founded by filmmaker, singer, actress, and storyteller Ndagire Mariam in 2020 and officially registered in 2025, NMM is a Ugandan creative powerhouse that has set its mark on the Ugandan Entertainment scene. We produce authentic Ugandan film, TV, and digital content, while training and launching the next generation of Media & Entertainment professionals. From prime-time TV content to blockbuster films and social media content, NMM delivers stories that entertain, inspire, and reflect on who we are.
